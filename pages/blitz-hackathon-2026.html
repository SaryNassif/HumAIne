A first chapter for HumAIne

The Blitz Hackathon marked HumAIne's first event. It was organized at Al Mawakeb School in collaboration with AMIC, the Al Mawakeb Innovation Center.

Participants worked through a focused AI challenge, developed their ideas into practical projects, and prepared to communicate what they had built. The event connected technical work with teamwork, problem-solving, and public presentation.

Winning projects

Meet the top three projects from the Blitz Hackathon and the students behind them.

1st place

Zaman Surfers

An interactive game that lets players explore different eras of the UAE through movement and cultural mini-games. Players learn about traditions, crafts, symbols, pearl diving, Arabic coffee etiquette, Sadu weaving, and other parts of Emirati heritage by playing rather than reading a textbook.

Team
Delara Darwish, Ward Al Khalili, and Omar Adi
Project focus
Interactive UAE cultural education

2nd place

SHWAY

An AI-assisted platform designed to preserve Emirati family recipes and the voices behind them. It listens while elders cook, translates informal measurements such as “shway” and “a handful” into usable quantities, saves each recipe in the speaker's voice, and turns family recipes into interactive cooking activities for children.

Team
Karim Hasan, Rayan Abu Qamar, Sara Zaabalawi, and Seyed Bastan
Project focus
AI-powered recipe and oral-history preservation

3rd place

Kanz

A cultural discovery platform that encourages people to visit and learn about UAE heritage sites. The concept combines an AI tour guide with location-based challenges, cultural quizzes, token rewards, and access to guided experiences, helping make local history more engaging and accessible.

Team
Maya Mousa, Adham Naserdine, and Ahmed Haroun
Project focus
AI-guided UAE heritage exploration
