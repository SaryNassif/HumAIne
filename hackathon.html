From challenge to product.

You are building two connected deliverables: a working AI product and the startup story that proves why it matters.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Unpack the revealed civic or scientific challenge.

  2. 02

    Define

    Choose a focused user problem your team can solve.

  3. 03

    Design

    Map the experience, data, and technical approach.

  4. 04

    Build

    Create and test your functional AI prototype.

  5. 05

    Pitch

    Explain the architecture, market, finances, and impact.

  6. 06

    Present

    Demo the product and defend your choices to judges.

Your final submission

A

Functional AI product

A working app, website, or AI tool—not only a concept.

B

Startup pitch deck

Architecture, competitor analysis, financial model, and impact.
