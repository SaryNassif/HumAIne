About us

The people behind the hackathon.

We’re a team of four students from school and university, brought together by one thing: a genuine love for technology.

We’ve always enjoyed taking part in tech events, competitions, and hackathons—building ideas, meeting ambitious people, and seeing what can be created in just a few intense days.

Then we had a simple thought: why only participate? Why not build one ourselves?

That idea became HumAIne, a hackathon created by students, for students, bringing together curious minds to build, experiment, collaborate, and turn ideas into something real.
