FAQs

Quick answers for first-time participants.

Do I need a team, or can I join solo?+

You can register your interest even if you do not yet have a complete team. Final team formation details will be shared with accepted participants.

Is prior AI experience required?+

No. Curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to learn matter most. Teams can combine technical, design, research, and presentation skills.

What is the cost to participate?+

Participation is 100% free. There is no registration or attendance fee.

What should I bring?+

Bring your laptop, charger, identification, and anything specified in the participant information sent before the event.

Who can participate?+

The hackathon is open to eligible high-school and university students across the UAE.
